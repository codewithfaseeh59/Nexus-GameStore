RETURN POLICY

Home / Return Policy

30-day returns

Not happy with your gear? Return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. Free standard shipping is not refunded on partial returns.

What qualifies

  • Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all accessories included.
  • Prebuilt PCs must be returned with all components and original packing.
  • Gift cards and digital codes are final sale.

How to return an item

  • Contact support with your order number and the item you want to return.
  • We email you a prepaid return label within 1 business day.
  • Pack the item securely and drop it off with the carrier.

Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive your return. Defective items are replaced at no cost.
