SHIPPING POLICY

Home / Shipping Policy

Order processing

Orders placed before 2 PM (Pacific) ship the same or next business day. Orders are processed Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. You receive a confirmation email once your order has shipped.

Delivery times and costs

MethodEstimated deliveryCost
Standard (US)3 to 7 business daysFree
Standard (International)7 to 14 business daysFree
Express1 to 3 business daysFrom $14.99

Delivery estimates start from the day your order ships, not the day it is placed.

International orders

We ship worldwide. Import duties, taxes and customs fees are set by your country and are the buyer’s responsibility. Local carriers may contact you to arrange delivery.

Tracking your order

Every shipment includes a tracking number. Use the Track Order page to follow your package.

Damaged or missing packages

If your package arrives damaged or does not arrive, contact us within 14 days of the delivery estimate and we will send a replacement or refund.
